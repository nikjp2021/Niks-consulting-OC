# Project Brief: Nik's Consulting

## Brand Personality
Innovative, Expert, Trustworthy

## Primary Color
Blue (#3B82F6)

## Accent Color
Green (#10B981)

## Target Audience
Startups and SMEs seeking AI-powered digital transformation solutions

## Core Conversion Goal
Get potential clients to book a free consultation or request a quote

## Tone
Professional yet approachable, technical but understandable

## Style Preferences
- Modern, clean design with subtle animations
- AI-first aesthetic
- Trust-building elements (case studies, testimonials, metrics)
- Mobile-responsive with emphasis on clear value proposition
